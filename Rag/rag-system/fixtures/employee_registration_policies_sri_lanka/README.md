# Employee Registration Policy Set — Sri Lanka

Synthetic policy corpus for a research RAG system. These documents are intended to validate employee-user registration before the ERP create-user action.

The policies cover:
- who may register a user;
- mandatory fields;
- Sri Lanka-only phone and location validation;
- email validation;
- names;
- gender and birth date;
- language and time zone;
- duplicate detection;
- decision handling and audit;
- combined validation order.
